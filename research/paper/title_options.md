# GreenScan Paper: Title Options

This document lists five scientifically appropriate paper titles for the GreenScan system. These titles avoid overclaiming terms such as "100% accuracy" or "exact lesion area" and focus on explainability, spatial activations, and severity estimation.

---

### Option 1 (Recommended Selected Title)
**"Explainable Deep Learning for Plant Disease Classification and Severity Estimation: A Spatial Attention Approach using Grad-CAM and EfficientNet-B0"**
- *Rationale:* This title captures the precise technical elements (EfficientNet-B0 and Grad-CAM), highlights the core contribution (explainable classification + relative severity estimation), and accurately positions it within the computer vision domain.

### Option 2
**"Bridging Deep Vision with Agronomic Decision Support: An Explainable Severity Indexing Framework for Solanaceous Crops"**
- *Rationale:* Focuses on the transition from deep learning outputs to actionable decision support (GSA score, dosage calculator, soil advisor) in agricultural fields.

### Option 5
**"Quantitative Leaf Health Assessment via Grad-CAM Spatial Activation Mapping: A Case Study on Tomato Foliar Pathology"**
- *Rationale:* Emphasizes the mathematical GSA scoring system derived from visual spatial hotspots on tomato leaves.

### Option 4
**"An Explainable Deep Learning Framework for Classifying and Assessing Severity of Solanaceous Pathologies"**
- *Rationale:* A general and highly defensible title that targets solanaceous crops (tomatoes) and disease tracking.

### Option 5
**"Interpretable Spatial Activation Mapping for Agronomic Disease Classification and Severity Indexing"**
- *Rationale:* Highlights the model interpretability aspect (explainability) as the primary research vehicle.
